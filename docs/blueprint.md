# **App Name**: SwingStats Pro

## Core Features:

- Round Data Entry: Allow users to log comprehensive round details including course rating, slope, par, gross score, and date, storing data locally for persistence.
- WHS Handicap Calculator: Automatically calculate the World Handicap System index based on the best 8 of the last 20 entered rounds, reflecting true playing ability.
- Course Handicap Calculator: Determine the course handicap for a selected golf course based on the user's WHS handicap index and the course's slope rating.
- Net Score & Basic Stats: Display net scores for individual rounds and provide basic overall statistics such as average gross and net scores across all recorded rounds.
- Offline Mode & Data Sync: Enable full offline usability, allowing users to enter and view data without internet access, with automatic synchronization to local storage when online.
- Handicap Trend Chart: Visualize the progression and trend of the user's handicap index over time through an interactive chart on the main dashboard.
- Personalized Performance Insight Tool: An AI tool that analyzes aggregated round data to automatically highlight a golfer's weakest areas of play and suggest potential focus areas for improvement.

## Style Guidelines:

- Color Palette Rationale: The chosen palette reflects the professional, analytical nature of golf stats and the rich greens of a golf course. With a 'Dark Mode Default', colors are selected for high contrast and readability.
- Primary Color: A refined, deep green (#339973) serves as the primary accent, evoking the lush fairways and focused precision of the sport, and stands out against the dark background.
- Background Color: A very dark, almost black, subtle green-tinted grey (#171F1C) forms the default dark mode background, providing a sophisticated canvas that minimizes eye strain.
- Accent Color: A bright, clear blue (#4DB8DE) is used for calls to action, highlights, and interactive elements, offering excellent contrast and guidance within the dark theme.
- All text uses 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif font known for its modern, neutral, and highly readable characteristics, making it ideal for presenting analytical data and a clean user experience.
- Utilize simple, outlined geometric icons. Employ golf-specific symbols where appropriate (e.g., flag, golf ball, club, course map) to maintain a relevant and clean visual language. Focus on clarity and ease of recognition.
- Implement a mobile-first, responsive layout with clear information hierarchy. Data tables and charts should be easily digestible on small screens, ensuring critical metrics are always accessible without clutter. Emphasize vertical scrolling and intuitive navigation paths.
- Subtle and functional animations for UI feedback, such as smooth transitions between dashboard views, concise loading indicators, and gentle haptic feedback on interactions (if supported by PWA platform), to enhance responsiveness without being distracting.
